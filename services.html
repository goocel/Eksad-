Automotive Resilience in Times of Uncertainty

The global economic slowdown and accompanying uncertainty have put pressure on various industries, including the automotive sector. Dealerships that adapt quickly are the ones that grow.

Consumers, now more cautious with their spending, are more likely to delay purchasing new vehicles. This has driven increased interest in the used car market, which is considered a more affordable alternative. This trend has resulted in a significant drop in new car sales across several key markets.

However, as seen in previous challenging times, the automotive industry has shown resilience. Car dealerships, which serve as the manufacturers' connection to customers, are adapting swiftly to these changing consumer preferences. They are shifting to digital strategies, leveraging technology to enhance operational efficiency and bring themselves closer to customers.

Digital Transformation in the Car-Buying Experience

Before the pandemic and current economic uncertainty, many consumers had already begun enjoying the convenience of exploring vehicles online. Manufacturer websites allowed consumers to "build their dream car," and interactive features like 360-degree views were becoming increasingly popular. But with the challenges posed by the economy and physical restrictions, dealerships began integrating more technology into their services.

Now, customers can complete more stages of the buying process online, including exploring financing and insurance options. With the use of apps and websites, dealerships are expanding their digital services. Customer interactions can be conducted through video calls, online consultations, or even scheduled visit services. This provides a more flexible and convenient experience for consumers while reducing the need for direct visits to showrooms.

Why Dealer Management Systems (DMS) Are a Vital Solution in Today’s Situation

A Dealer Management System (DMS) has become an essential tool for automotive dealerships in facing these changes and challenges. This system not only helps dealerships optimize daily operations but also enables them to respond to market shifts quickly and effectively. Here are some reasons why DMS is crucial in today’s climate:

Contact our team

  1. Efficiency in Inventory Management

    In an economic situation demanding tight cost control, a DMS helps dealerships monitor inventory in real time. With efficient inventory management, dealerships can avoid overstocking and understocking issues, thereby reducing operational costs.

  2. More Personalized Customer Experience

    A DMS enables dealerships to store customer data and interaction history. This information allows dealerships to provide a more personalized experience for each consumer, creating loyalty and increasing the likelihood of repeat purchases.

  3. Data-Driven Decision-Making

    With the data provided by a DMS, dealerships can monitor purchasing trends and adjust sales strategies with greater accuracy. For instance, if demand for used cars increases, dealerships can focus on promotional strategies that align with this need.

  4. Adapting to Digitalization Trends

    A DMS supports integration with various digital platforms, allowing dealerships to manage online interactions with customers. With more consumers beginning their purchasing journey online, the ability to provide a seamless digital experience has become a competitive advantage.

With a reliable Dealer Management System, automotive dealerships can better adapt to the current economic climate and stay competitive amid uncertainty.

Six Modules. One Dealership

From the showroom floor to the workshop bay, every ProDMS module works on the same data in real time.

Module 01

Sales

The Vehicle Sales module provides functionality required for effective vehicle sales management and automation of invoicing and payment collection processes. The module consists of the following components: Vehicle Sales Order, Automatic or Manual Overwrite Matching between Sales Order and vehicle stock, Pre Delivery Inspection and Delivery Orders, Vehicle Registration Number processing and Invoicing.

Module 02

Workshop & Services Management

The Service Management module manages activities related to workshop and service department, starting from service appointment, work orders, parts & oil usage, technician/labor working hours to invoicing and cashier operations. The module tracked customer and vehicles service histories, including those done with third party vendors. The system will automatically generate invoice for each itemized parts, oil and labor involved in a work order.

Module 03

Part Purchasing

The Parts Purchasing module provides facilities to help your company track various parts in multi site and warehouse location. Information collected from each counter related to the used parts for service and the back order which is used to make requisition. System will check the availability and process the approval for Purchase Order. Data for PO can be imported and used with Manufacturer Parts Ordering System.

Module 04

Real-Time Updated Inventory Information

The DMS provides real-time updated inventory information, enabling dealers to monitor stock levels for vehicles, parts, and other equipment accurately. This feature helps dealers manage availability and prevent overstocking or stockouts.

Module 05

Integrated CRM with Marketing Automation Features

The DMS includes an integrated Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system with marketing automation features, allowing dealers to manage customer relationships and execute marketing campaigns automatically. This feature enhances customer engagement and builds stronger loyalty.

Module 06

Integrated Appointment Management System for Sales and Service

With an integrated appointment management system, dealers can efficiently schedule sales and service appointments. This feature optimizes time management and increases customer satisfaction through structured and timely arrangements.

Human Capital Information System

A cloud-based human capital management application tailored for modern business needs. With multi-platform advantages, Prohace – HCIS offers ease of use, lightweight performance, cost efficiency, fast report generation, convenient data migration, and seamless integration with your company’s existing systems. The ideal choice for effective and efficient human capital management.

Four Modules. Every HR Process

From core HR and attendance to payroll and performance, all in a single application.

Module 01

Core HR & Movement

Prohace offers nearly all processes needed for efficient human capital management. It even accommodates multi-company or group company concepts and outsourcing management, simplifying large-scale administration. Advanced access settings include multi-role configurations, menu access, button access, and data access based on company, location, and grade. This ensures users can only access data according to their authority, enhancing data security and compliance.

Organizational structures, organizational templates, and comprehensive employee data can be easily set up or migrated via Excel. Approval workflows can be customized to various levels, and the career movement feature covers promotion, demotion, rotation, termination, and cross-company movement.

Module 02

Comprehensive Attendance

Prohace offers ease in managing employee attendance and contract workers (outsourcing), including clock-in, clock-out, leave requests, permissions, sick leave, business trips, overtime, and attendance data correction. Employees can easily use the attendance feature through a mobile application available in Android and iOS versions. Mobile attendance is equipped with Geo-Tagged and Face Detection, providing real-time location reports and photo verification.

WFO (Work from Office), WFH (Work from Home), and Shift schedules can be easily arranged via Excel upload or per individual employee. Attendance information is integrated with payroll calculations through the system, ensuring accuracy and efficiency.

Module 03

Easy Payroll and Tax

Prohace features comprehensive and user-friendly payroll and tax calculation management. Component parameters and formulas can be easily configured following the latest regulations. Payroll components can be managed using batch Excel uploads, individual component updates, or automatically calculated by the system. Payroll calculations are connected with attendance data, social security (BPJS), employee loans, and scheduled repayment settings.

The calculation process is straightforward and fast. The system will automatically display which employees need recalculation. Users can perform calculations on all employees or individually. Tax calculations cover employee taxes and contract worker taxes, and Form 1721 can be automatically generated by the system. For holding companies, calculations or monitoring of all subsidiaries can be easily done within a single application.

Module 04

Performance Management

A 360° performance appraisal feature is available, including self-assessment, supervisors, subordinates, peers, or specific employees. Questionnaires can be easily inputted through the Employee Self Service (ESS), with scores automatically calculated by the system and comparable among reviewers. The final score can be adjusted as needed by authorized users.

To provide solutions for tracking employee competency development, there is a People Development feature covering the assessment process, development programs, monitoring, and evaluation.

The ideal choice for effective and efficient human capital management.

Prohace is lightweight, cost-efficient, and integrates seamlessly with your company’s existing systems.

Six Solutions. One Security Partner

From endpoints and privileged accounts to round-the-clock monitoring, every layer of your business is covered.

  1. Unified Endpoint Management (UEM)

    This refers to a comprehensive approach to managing and securing an organization's endpoint devices (such as smartphones, laptops, desktops, tablets, and IoT devices). The Unified Endpoint Management (UEM) platform provides tools to manage device configurations, security policies, applications, and user access across various types of devices within an organization.

  2. Privileged Access Management (PAM)

    This refers to a set of cybersecurity practices and technologies designed to manage and monitor access to critical systems and data by privileged users (such as system administrators, IT staff, or other high-level users with elevated access permissions). Privileged Access Management (PAM) solutions help secure, control, and monitor privileged accounts to reduce the risk of data breaches and cyberattacks.

  3. Bug Bounty

    It is a program offered by organizations, companies, or platforms to encourage individuals (often ethical hackers, security researchers, or developers) to find and report security vulnerabilities or bugs in their software or systems. In return for discovering and reporting these issues, participants are rewarded with money, recognition, or other incentives.

  4. Vulnerability Assessment (VA)

    Refers to the process of identifying, evaluating, and prioritizing security vulnerabilities within a system or network. It is a crucial part of cybersecurity to ensure that an organization's infrastructure remains secure. Vulnerability Assessment (VA) involves scanning systems to detect weaknesses, determining their severity levels, and implementing fixes or mitigation controls to prevent exploitation.

  5. Security Operations Center (SOC)

    It is a centralized facility within an organization that is responsible for monitoring, detecting, responding to, and mitigating security threats and incidents. It is a vital part of an organization's cybersecurity strategy.

  6. Antimalware

    Refers to software designed to detect, prevent, and remove malicious software (malware) from computers or networks. Malware can include viruses, worms, trojans, ransomware, spyware, adware, and other types of harmful programs that can compromise system and data security.

Cybersecurity has become a mandatory need for every business that wants to survive and grow in the digital era.
